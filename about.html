About AI Words

A small, independent project that puts AI's text output next to ours.

Why this exists

Every few months a company announces another enormous token number, and each one is hard to picture on its own. Plotted together and converted to words, they tell a clear story: in under four years, machine-generated text went from a novelty to a volume comparable to everything humans say and write.

AI Words keeps that picture in one place, sourced and up to date, so anyone can check the numbers instead of trusting a headline.

Who runs it

AI Words is built and maintained by Siddhant Dubey, a software engineer in Bangalore who works on distributed systems and infrastructure. It is not affiliated with Google, OpenAI, OpenRouter or any other company whose figures appear here.

Get in touch

For corrections or new sources, open an issue on GitHub. For anything else, reach Siddhant on LinkedIn.

Using the data

The dataset is open. Download data.json and reuse it in articles, slides or research. A link back to aiwords.si is appreciated.
