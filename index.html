How many words does AI write per day?

Since ChatGPT launched in November 2022, AI systems have gone from a rounding error to a volume of text close to everything people say and write. This site tracks that shift from the companies' own numbers.

AI words per day, by source

Each line is one company's own disclosure, converted to words per day. The dashed line is the estimated output of every person on Earth. The jump is four to five orders of magnitude in about three years.

Reading the log scale

Each gridline is ten times the one below. Switch to linear and everything before 2025 flattens to zero, which is the story in one click.

Why the curve is so steep

Reasoning models and agents use far more tokens per request than 2023 chatbots did, so part of the rise is more work per question, not only more people.

What isn't counted

Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, xAI and self-hosted open models publish few comparable figures. Real global volume is higher than any single line.

Share of new web articles that are mostly AI-written

Graphite sampled English-language articles from Common Crawl and ran them through several AI detectors. Machine-written articles crossed 50% around November 2024 and have held there since.

Why it flattened

Graphite links the plateau to AI articles performing poorly in search. In its companion study, about 86% of top Google results were human-written.

Detector limits

Detectors produce false positives and miss AI drafts that people edited. Treat the line as a trend that is accurate to a few points.

Volume and share differ

Most AI text stays inside chats and apps and never reaches the public web. That is how volume can grow a thousandfold while the web share holds at half.

Common questions

How many words does AI generate per day?

Public figures put it in the tens of trillions. Google alone reported 3.2 quadrillion tokens a month in May 2026, roughly 79 trillion words a day. That counts prompts as well as replies, so it is an upper bound on text actually written.

How many words do humans produce per day?

About 100 trillion words a day, spoken and written, across everyone on Earth. The estimate comes from OpenAI CEO Sam Altman in February 2024.

How much of the web is AI-written?

Graphite's Common Crawl study found mostly AI-written articles reached about half of new English web articles by late 2024, and the share has held near 50% since.

How many words is a token?

In English, one token is about three quarters of a word, or roughly four characters. This site converts every token figure at 0.75 words per token.
